Infrastructure Development

Robust infrastructure forms the backbone of economic prosperity. Our civil engineering teams execute heavy foundation projects, grade-separated highways, retaining walls, and site earthworks designed to endure high environmental stress.

  • Civil Engineering Works: Reinforced concrete bridges, culverts, and structural retaining walls.
  • Roads and Pathways: Heavy-duty asphalt and concrete pavements built for dense traffic.
  • Site Development: Bulk earth excavation, stormwater management networks, and grading.

Renovation & Remodeling

Sector 06

Construction Management

Experienced construction management saves time, eliminates budgetary waste, and preserves design integrity. We act as your owner's representative, overseeing contractors, verifying material test reports, and enforcing strict site safety compliance.

  • Project Planning: Milestone schedules, critical path mapping, and cashflow modeling.
  • Budget Estimation: Comprehensive Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with market rate benchmarking.
  • Contractor Coordination: Tendering, subcontractor vetting, and contract enforcement.
  • Site Supervision: Daily certified engineering oversight and progress logs.
  • Quality Inspection: Independent laboratory cube testing, slump tests, and snag audits.
