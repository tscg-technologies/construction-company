Project Overview

Architectural & Engineering Blueprint

This illustrative multi-storey residential project highlights modern living aesthetics paired with sustainable structural engineering. The project includes reinforced concrete framework, energy-efficient glazing, seismic resistance, rainwater harvesting integration, and community recreational spaces.

Materials & Construction Approach

High-grade M30 & M40 concrete, Fe500D steel reinforcement, acoustic insulated glass units, low-VOC interior coatings, sustainable vitrified tiling.

Construction Process & Safety Compliance

Execution adhered to a strict 5-stage milestone progression, starting with continuous piling, post-pour ultrasonic concrete testing, moisture-proof chemical membranes, and comprehensive fire-suppression dry runs before final commissioning.
