Stage-Gate Methodology

The 8 Stages of Our Construction Cycle

A comprehensive breakdown of how each phase is executed from initial client handshake to final keys handover.

01
💬

Initial Consultation

We meet with clients to understand functional goals, stylistic preferences, target square footage, budgetary parameters, and initial scheduling expectations.

02
🗺️

Site Assessment

Geotechnical soil testing, topographical contour surveys, groundwater table analysis, and local municipal zoning verification to establish structural feasibility.

03
📐

Architectural Planning

Development of conceptual floor plans, 3D structural BIM models, elevation renders, and complete mechanical, electrical, and plumbing (MEP) schematics.

04
📋

Design & Estimation

Detailed Bill of Quantities (BOQ), material specifications, fixed milestone cost schedules, and transparent procurement contracts with zero hidden clauses.

05
📅

Project Scheduling

Establishing Critical Path Method (CPM) timelines, labor mobilization rosters, heavy equipment scheduling, and regulatory permit submissions.

06
🏗️

Construction Execution

Excavation, foundation piling, RCC framework pouring, brick masonry, waterproofing, MEP rough-ins, and ongoing quality supervision.

07
🔬

Quality Inspection

Comprehensive non-destructive concrete testing, plumbing pressure tests, electrical load distribution audits, and snag-list rectification.

08
🔑

Final Handover

Final client walkthrough, handover of as-built architectural drawings, equipment operational manuals, municipal occupancy certificates, and keys.

📡

Project Communication

Clients receive bi-weekly photographic progress reports, milestone expenditure updates, and direct access to their assigned Senior Project Manager.

🛡️

Safety Considerations

Mandatory daily toolbox briefings, full PPE enforcement, scaffolding stability checks, perimeter nets, and designated site safety officers at every build.

⚖️

Quality Management

Independent third-party laboratory cube tests for concrete, batch chemical certificates for reinforcement steel, and waterproof flood testing for all wet areas.
