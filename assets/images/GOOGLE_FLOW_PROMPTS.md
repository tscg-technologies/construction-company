# GOOGLE FLOW PROMPT GUIDE: 10 IMAGE ASSETS FOR CONSTRUCTION COMPANY WEBSITE

To ensure your generated images match the premium corporate aesthetic of the website (Deep Navy Blue, Warm Architectural Lighting, Construction Safety Yellow/Amber accents, Concrete Grey, and Modern Engineering Precision), use the master style guide and the individual prompts below in Google Flow.

---

## 🎨 Master Aesthetic Style Parameters for Google Flow
- **Style / Tone:** Premium architectural photography, hyper-realistic 8k, Hasselblad H6D-100c medium format camera, natural daylight with golden hour / blue hour warmth.
- **Color Harmony:** Deep navy, steel blue, concrete textures, warm amber highlights, high contrast, clean architectural geometry.
- **Composition:** Wide-angle structural perspective, straight vertical lines (tilt-shift lens effect), crisp depth of field, professional editorial quality.
- **Negative Prompts:** Blurry, low-resolution, cartoon, 3d render distortion, exaggerated fantasy sci-fi, chaotic debris, unsafe construction practices, distorted hands/faces.

---
